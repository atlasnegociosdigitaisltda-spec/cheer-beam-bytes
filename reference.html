    How do the shorts offers work?

    Simply choose the offer you prefer before adding the product to your cart. Available options may include 1, 2, 4, or more pairs of shorts, as displayed on the product page.

    What are the shorts made of?

    Made from a blend of polyester and spandex, these shorts combine lightweight, soft, breathable fabric with stretch that moves with your body.

    Do the shorts have a built-in lining?

    Yes! The shorts feature a built-in inner lining for added coverage while you move.

    Are they suitable for workouts?

    Yes. They’re ideal for gym workouts, running, walking, yoga, or comfortable everyday wear.
