# Reorganizar o FAQ

## Objetivo
Ordenar as perguntas pela jornada de compra: primeiro características e uso do produto, depois oferta, entrega e troca.

## Nova ordem
1. What are the shorts made from?
2. Do the shorts have a built-in liner?
3. Are they suitable for workouts?
4. Can the pockets hold my phone?
5. How do the shorts offers work?
6. How long does shipping take?
7. Can I exchange my order if the size does not fit?
